🌍 Application & Problem Solving

Term 6: Mathematics in the Real World

During Term 6, pupils should apply previously taught mathematical skills in meaningful and engaging real-life contexts.


The emphasis should be on problem solving, independence, communication, decision making and preparation for adulthood.


For Belonging learners this may involve participation, anticipation and noticing mathematical changes. For Achievement and Celebration learners this may involve budgeting, measuring, planning and solving real-world problems.

📸 Assessment Focus

Term 6 assessment should focus on application rather than isolated skills.


  • Application of mathematical learning
  • Problem solving
  • Communication
  • Independence
  • Decision making
  • Generalisation of skills
  • Preparation for adulthood outcomes

Key Principle:
Term 6 is not about teaching more mathematics. It is about helping pupils use the mathematics they already know in meaningful, enjoyable and authentic situations.
