Comparison Strand

Comparing amounts, quantities, collections, sizes and values. This strand develops pupils' understanding of more, fewer, equal and comparison language.

Level 4
I can compare two groups up to 5
Achievement

Target Breakdown

  • Looks at two groups.
  • Identifies more and fewer.
  • Matches equal quantities.

Emerging

  • Compares groups with support.
  • Indicates larger group.

Developing

  • Compares independently.
  • Uses simple comparison language.

Secure

  • Explains which group has more.
  • Finds equal groups.

Suggested Activities

  • Snack comparisons.
  • Compare towers.
  • Counter collections.

Vocabulary

more fewer same equal

Common Misconceptions

  • Bigger objects always mean more.
  • Focuses on size rather than quantity.
